SPL Offline Training App

The SPL training record card as an app that works without a signal. Instructors record each student's training at the launch point, even on airfields with no mobile coverage, and the record stays complete from first flight to Sailplane Pilot Licence.

  • Works fully offline, so a weak signal at the launch point never holds up a brief, debrief or even inflight records.
  • Each exercise on the SPL record card recorded and signed off flight by flight.
  • See where a student is up to before the next flight, instantly at a glance.
  • No lost, damp or illegible paper cards.
  • Portable, verified, and always accessible training records.
For SPL instructors & students

Commercial Software

Bootfair Expense Tracking

  • Fair Ledger is a per-event profit tracker for craft fair and market sellers. A seller logs each fair’s pitch fee, mileage, hours and what they packed. Afterwards they count what came home, or import their card reader’s CSV. The app then shows real profit, £ per hour, and a Rebook / Review / Drop verdict for each fair.
  • Profit is revenue minus cost of goods, pitch and application fees, other costs, and mileage. Mileage defaults to 55p a mile (HMRC’s rate) and can be changed per seller or per fair.
  • Revenue comes from recorded takings if there are any. Otherwise it’s worked out from the pack list as (items out − items back) × price.
  • Verdict:
    • Drop if the fair lost money.
    • Review if it came in under the target £/hour (default £12).
    • Otherwise Rebook.
  • Money is stored as whole pence throughout.
For Craft, Maker and Boot Fair sellers
